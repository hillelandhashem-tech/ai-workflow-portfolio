01 / A system in use

Digital marketing agency
Live with clients

Closing the
lead-feedback gap.

Delivering a lead is only part of the job. Understanding whether it was a good lead is where the next decision begins.

Smaller clients without an active CRM had no central place to review inquiries from Google and Meta advertising. Consistent feedback was difficult to collect, leaving a gap between campaign activity and what clients knew about their prospective customers.

I built a connected workflow that brings those inquiries into one Google Sheet, adds useful context, and gives clients a straightforward way to tell us what happened next.

My contribution

I personally designed and implemented the workflow: API connections, webhooks, custom GoHighLevel workflows, and qualification criteria based on each client's ideal customer profile. I used our existing agentic system to carry out the build without another team member implementing it.

The environment

The shared OpenClaw harness was developed by our CEO with my operational input. This case study covers the workflow I built within that environment.

02 / Behind the sheet

Connected systems.
One clear place to work.

The integrations carry the complexity. The client gets a familiar interface.

01 →

Capture

Webhooks and integrations bring website form submissions and phone-call information into GoHighLevel.

02 →

Add context

For clients using call-tracking tools, I ensure API access to recordings and transcripts. Otherwise, GoHighLevel records and transcribes calls. The workflow adds summaries and source details.

03 →

Qualify

Agents assess transcript evidence against client-agreed criteria, producing a percentage or 1–5 qualification rating.

04 →

Review together

Clients see leads, context, and ratings in Google Sheets. They add comments or revise ratings using their firsthand knowledge.

05 ↺

Return feedback

Scheduled API jobs send client-confirmed qualified leads to Google Ads and Meta for campaign optimization. Other disqualification feedback guides the team's improvements.

The loop closes: client feedback informs landing-page copy, form questions, and campaign decisions. Qualified-lead conversions return to advertising platforms with attribution details.

03 / The client experience

A Google Sheet.
By design.

Clients shouldn't need to learn another platform just to tell us whether a lead was useful.

Shared access, comments, and straightforward editing make participation easy. Offline viewing and editing are also available when configured; connected updates resume online.

04 / How I work

Automation with judgment.
Partnership with people.

Knowing which tool to use matters. So does knowing when to listen, check, or involve another person.

Hear the client first.

I listen openly before presenting data or an explanation. Their understanding of their customers helps shape the qualification criteria and the decisions we make together.

Check the work.

Scheduled connectivity checks and pipeline tests monitor the workflow. Human spot checks review transcript accuracy and whether qualification ratings make sense.

Choose tools for the task.

I work across AI platforms and connected systems, selecting tools for code, context, analysis, and implementation. Agents support execution; people contribute expertise and accountability.

Platforms and tools I work with

AI and agentic environments

Codex, Claude, Claude Design, Gemini, our shared OpenClaw environment, and other AI tools. I combine platforms according to the work rather than treating one model as the answer to every problem.

Connected operations

GoHighLevel, Google Workspace, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Search Atlas, call-tracking tools such as CallRail and CallTrackingMetrics, and many more.
